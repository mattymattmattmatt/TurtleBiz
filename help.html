Say hello
How do I order?

Explore the collection and add items to your bag. From your bag, open an order enquiry in your email app or copy the details into an email. We confirm product availability, delivery costs and payment arrangements with you before an order is placed.

Can I pay on the website?

Online payment isn't available here. Building a bag or opening an email draft does not take payment or confirm an order. Please don't include card details in your message.

What currency are the prices in?

All displayed prices are in Australian dollars (AUD). Your bag shows the item subtotal. Delivery is confirmed separately.

Can I ask about shipping or a return?

Yes. Contact us with your destination for a delivery enquiry, or your order details if you need help with a return. We'll help with the next step.

Where does my bag go when I close the page?

Your bag and saved favourites stay in this browser when browser storage is available. They aren't linked to an account or shared between devices. Private browsing or clearing site data may remove them.

How do I play Shell Sprint?

Open the arcade and select Start a round. Move your turtle with ↑ and ↓ or W and S, drag on the game area, or use the on-screen controls. Collect gold coins, dodge the jellyfish, and see what you can score in 30 seconds. You can pause at any time.

Can I turn the animations off?

Use Pause motion in the footer. We also follow your device's reduced-motion setting. Arcade rounds only start when you choose to play, and music only starts when you press play.

Where is Turtle Chat?

You’ll find Turtle Chat on the homepage, just below Turtle Biz Radio. The original conversation is still there. Read earlier messages or leave a public message of up to 150 characters; no account is needed. For personal details or order questions, use the contact page.

How can I hear the music?

Visit Turtle Biz Radio on the home page. Choose one of the three original tracks, then press play. Playback starts only when you ask it to.

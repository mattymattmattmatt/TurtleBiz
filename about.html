OUR STORY

Small beginnings.
A world of character.

Turtle Biz began in 2024 with a simple idea: share a love of turtles through things that make people smile. That little idea grew into a collection of curious gifts, desk companions, and everyday finds.

Then came the music. And the games. Because a little corner of the internet should be somewhere you actually want to spend time.

Whether you're finding a gift, putting on a track, or chasing a high score, you're very welcome here.
